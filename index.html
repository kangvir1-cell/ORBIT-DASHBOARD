ORBIT Workflow Guide

Input: Scan barcode or enter ASIN.
Product: Product details can be fetched through the approved product-data integration in a later build.
Task: Pending until a TID is entered. Completed after reconciliation.
Ageing: Numeric days only; colours are 1 Green, 2 Yellow, 3 Amber, 4+ Red.
Retention: Completed records leave the active dashboard 7 days after completion. Pending records remain active until completed.
Site Code: Each site's view is scoped to its selected site code.
